Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a check-up and cleaning every 6 months for most patients. However, some may need more frequent visits based on their oral health needs.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Our staff will help you understand your coverage and maximize your benefits.

What if I have dental anxiety?

We understand dental anxiety is common. We offer sedation options and create a calm, comfortable environment. Let us know about your concerns, and we'll work with you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans to make dental care affordable. Speak with our financial coordinator to find the best option for you.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us immediately at +1 (919) 409-7202. We offer same-day emergency appointments and will provide guidance over the phone if needed.

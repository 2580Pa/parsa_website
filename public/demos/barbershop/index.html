Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend booking ahead via WhatsApp to guarantee your preferred time slot.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, and mobile payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay.

How long does a haircut take?

A classic haircut typically takes 30-45 minutes. Premium packages allow about 60-75 minutes for the full experience.

Do you offer kids' haircuts?

Yes! We welcome young clients aged 5 and up. Kids' cuts are $25 and our barbers are great with children.
